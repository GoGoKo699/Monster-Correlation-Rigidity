# Contribution verdict and direct prior-theorem comparison

Research assessment, 26 September 2026. This is not an abstract, introduction, conclusion, or submission document. It contains no private publication planning. The comparison concerns the bounded quantitative identification theorem, not unrestricted moonshine uniqueness.

## 1. Decision after the directed comparison

**Retain the uniform coefficient-to-structure criterion as the principal bounded contribution. Attribute its exact classification endpoint and general variational geometry to prior work. Do not open another special-subtheory campaign merely to avoid deciding the current result's scope.**

Within the primary sources and theorem statements inspected below, no result was found with the same input (arbitrary actual near-extremal normalized primary fields, with uniform coefficient bounds) and the same output (exact orthogonal Ising subalgebras and ambient moonshine identification). This is a scoped comparison, not a certification that no equivalent statement exists anywhere or a claim of being first.

The defensible contribution is a **quantitative, source-based identification theorem**. It is not a new Ising-pair classification, a new general theorem about extrema of metrized algebras, a new generic Morse lemma, or a solution of bare-class uniqueness. Its proof is comparatively short because it uses deep established inputs. That can be useful, but it also creates a significance risk: a specialist may judge the result a modest corollary rather than a major structural advance. Adding more recorded constants or unrelated applications does not resolve that judgment.

The bounded priority obligation is closed at the evidence level represented by this dossier: the nearest inspected statements have been compared by hypotheses and conclusions, generic ingredients have been credited, and no direct overlap has been identified in that corpus. Scholarly priority always remains revisable if a closer source is supplied. It is not appropriate to repeat the same indefinite keyword search as a substitute for a contribution decision.

## 2. Exact object of comparison

Fix an exact simple unitary rational C2-cofinite holomorphic VOA of CFT type with c=24 and V1=0. Given two real normalized weight-two primaries, their two self-three-point coefficients and one cross-two-point coefficient satisfy explicit sufficient inequalities. These force nearby exact Ising directions, then an exact orthogonal pair, and finally the ambient VOA isomorphism type by the prior theorem of Abe–Lam–Yamada.

Uniformity means the constants do not depend on unknown multiplication coordinates or on an already supplied nearby exact axis. It does not mean the class axioms are inferred from the coefficients. Failure of the sufficient inequalities is not an exclusion test. In particular, the theorem has meaningful conditional content without proving existence of a hypothetical competitor to moonshine.

## 4. What the new completion work adds

Two auxiliary issues are settled in `research/sharpness_and_calibration.md`:

* The exponent 1/2 in value-to-nearest-axis distance is optimal, using an explicit curve inside the actual moonshine theory. This is the familiar quadratic-loss phenomenon realized in the relevant class, not a new generic exponent law.
* Seven scalar coefficients for two exact real weight-two fields determine their primary-projected normalized inputs. Certified interval arithmetic can propagate uncertainty in these coefficients. The identities are algebraic and do not relax the ambient axioms or grade/reality assumptions.

These strengthen the precision and usability of the bounded statement. They should be presented as proof-completion and scope results, not as separate breakthroughs intended to inflate the central claim. The old dyadic thresholds are sufficient constants, not optimal ones. The optimality result concerns field-distance extraction, not the optimal possible decision region for identifying a VOA.

## 5. Search procedure and its limitations

The search was directed from the exact source inputs and their reference chains, not only from numerical constants. Starting points were Sakuma and ALY. The variational/idempotent search led to Tkachev, whose references led to the universal axial-algebra work. Exact-pair successors and framed-characterization predecessors were inspected, and the terminology match “stable axial algebras” was checked against its actual definition. The current reconstruction paper was read for background and whether the broader claim had already changed.

Additional combinations of “Griess,” “Ising vectors,” “near-extremal,” “quantitative,” “approximate idempotents,” and “axial” returned many unrelated lattice-spin, computational optimization, and chemical-reagent results. Those are not evidence for originality. An unmatched query is not a proof that no source exists. This is not a bibliometric census, a complete crawl of all citing papers, or a claim to have read every source's entire proof.

## 6. Scientific gap assessment

No new blocking error was found in the core Q1–Q4 proof at this pass's interface depth. The important points checked were: the positive-energy cyclic Virasoro module; the distinction between exact Ising highest weights and their ambient descendants; uniform local growth without Monster multiplicities; global entry without compactness of a moduli space; singular-product orthogonality; and the exact ambient hypotheses at ALY.

The added calibration theorem explicitly handles stress contamination without claiming uncertain grade or uncertain PCT reality. The sharpness example has an actual known-theory embedding; it is not a three-dimensional algebra asserted to be an alternative full CFT.

The imported classification proofs have not been independently re-proved here. Importing correctly stated mathematics is part of a mathematical proof, not an unfinished obligation to reproduce all foundational papers. Source misapplication would be a blocking gap; the source interface matrix records what was checked and what remains an inherited theorem.

The next stage for the chosen bounded contribution is integrated reproducibility and an adversarial read of this assembled chain. Automatic first-Ising existence, extendability in every unknown candidate, and approximate-VOA axioms are not required lemmas for the stated conditional result. They should not be silently demanded, nor silently claimed solved.
