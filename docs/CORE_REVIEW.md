## Which feature survives the comparison?

The lattice comparison shares the information thresholds but not the exceptional cancellation or the saturated cubic extremum. Its primary thermal response is already nonzero at weight4; every nonzero weight-two Virasoro component has central charge an integer at least1. The known moonshine theory has cancellation through11 and c=1/2 Ising components.

Therefore the next small selection question is not another tomography constant. It is whether the physically restricted holomorphic/current-free/extremal setting forces saturation of the unitarity bound, and what additional hypotheses would be needed to reconstruct the Griess algebra from the resulting structure. A positive answer is not assumed. Existence of one Ising component alone must not be silently upgraded to full moonshine uniqueness.
