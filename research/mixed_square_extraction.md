# Extracting an Ising component from a mixed square: a constructive test and its obstruction

**Date:** 26 September 2026. **Read baseline:** `09e3f98ad79034eefab43f4fb9ea432368fc19e7`.
**Additive branch:** `research/mixed-square-extraction`.

## 1. Result: replace a guessed endpoint by a tested local structure

The preceding Potts landscape calculation used an already supplied Ising endpoint. This continuation removes that endpoint from the finite extraction: a charge-4/5 component and one suitably coupled field generate a small algebra whose cubic identifies its Ising directions. One mixed square first extracts a candidate commuting component. Checking its products and the residual two-dimensional cubic distinguishes the Ising-producing case from a second, superficially similar case.

There is also a definite obstruction. The known moonshine VOA contains a ternary-code sub-VOA of central charge 12/5 with a five-dimensional weight-two algebra. It has a closed four-dimensional algebra generated by a Potts component and one mixed field, yet it has **no Ising vector anywhere in its complete weight-two space**. The exact idempotent equations give 32 solutions, all of charges different from 1/2. Adding the complementary ambient stress tensor does not supply one either.

Thus small mixed closure is not sufficient. In the two concrete examples, the mixed-square construction returns commuting components of charges **6/7** and **8/5**, respectively. Both are actual Virasoro vectors acting with weight 4/3 on the mixed field, but only the first local algebra produces the Ising directions considered here.

The embedding and both product tables are prior results of the sources below. The present contribution is an explicit endpoint-free extraction, a general formula for the candidate companion, and an exact obstruction/certificate clarifying what must be forced in an unknown candidate. It is not a classification of the whole c24 theory, a new proof of the source embeddings, or a claim of cleared novelty. The original assumptions still have not been shown to force a first Ising component or its orthogonal partner.

## 2. Exact class and conventions

The project investigates simple unitary, rational, C2-cofinite, holomorphic VOAs of CFT type with c=24 and V1=0. These are declared restrictions, not universal physical necessities or an established RG mechanism. The local extraction in section 3 needs only the positive-real, current-free VOA setting with an actual simple Virasoro component of charge 4/5; it does not need the exceptional trace spectrum, holomorphicity, or a Monster action.

Use the real PCT-fixed space B=V2, the positive invariant metric, and the FULL weight-two product a*b=a_(1)b. The ambient conformal vector obeys omega*a=2a and ||omega||^2=12. Removing its direction is necessary when discussing primary cubic extrema, but we do not remove it from products in this note.

An element e with e*e=2e is a Virasoro vector in this setting, with central charge 2||e||^2. Positivity identifies a charge-1/2 such vector with the simple Ising VOA L(1/2,0). A positive scalar called a candidate charge below is NOT a Virasoro central charge until the idempotent equation has actually been verified.

Every structure used in the extraction can be checked by exact multiplication and inner products. No approximate-VOA theorem, field-preparation procedure, practical optimizer, or sampling-cost bound is being supplied.

## 3. What one mixed square determines in an arbitrary candidate

Let u be a real simple charge-4/5 Virasoro vector and let y in B satisfy

u*y=(2/3)y,  n=||y||^2>0.

The Virasoro minimal-model fusion rule is

L(4/5,2/3) times L(4/5,2/3) -> L(4/5,0), L(4/5,2/3), L(4/5,3).

It follows from the (1,3) fusion rule in M(5,6), not from an assumed Potts extension. Positive Virasoro modes annihilate y: their possible targets are V1=0 and the vacuum, with the latter coefficient zero because y is perpendicular to u. Thus y is a highest-weight vector for this Virasoro algebra.

At ambient weight two, the internal weight-three channel cannot occur. In this positive current-free setting, 0<=R_u<=2I on B: ||u_(0)b||^2=2<b,R_u b> gives nonnegativity, and applying the same fact to omega-u gives the upper bound. Vacuum-module descendants allow the u direction at eigenvalue 2; the rest of y*y lies in the eigenvalue-0 and eigenvalue-2/3 spaces. There is only one internal eigenvalue-2 vacuum descendant, because the ambient vacuum is unique and V1=0.

Metric invariance fixes the u component:

<u,y*y>=(2/3)n,  ||u||^2=2/5,

so y*y=(5n/3)u + d + h, with R_u d=0 and R_u h=(2/3)h. Therefore

$$d(y)=y*y-\frac32u*(y*y)+\frac{10n}{3}u.\tag{1}$$

This formula isolates the commuting weight-two component without diagonalizing a large operator. Its overlap with the complementary stress tensor is

$$\langle d,\omega-u\rangle=\frac43n>0.\tag{2}$$

Thus d is never zero. Define

$$v(y)=\frac{4n}{3\|d\|^2}d,\qquad
\chi(y)=2\|v(y)\|^2=\frac{32n^2}{9\|d\|^2}.\tag{3}$$

These quantities are unchanged by rescaling y. They obey R_u v=0 and

$$\frac{\langle y,v*y\rangle}{\|y\|^2}=\frac43.\tag{4}$$

Equation (4) is a Rayleigh quotient, **not** automatically an eigenvector identity. Nor does (3) imply v*v=2v. The two residuals that must be checked are

$$v*v-2v,\qquad v*y-\frac43y.\tag{5}$$

When they vanish, v is a real Virasoro vector commuting with u, with actual central charge chi, and y has v-weight 4/3. Cauchy-Schwarz in (2) gives 0<chi<=116/5 in the ambient c24 setting, but does not force the smaller desired value.

### A small unitary gap test, conditional on those residuals

If (5) holds and chi<=9/10, the unitary Virasoro classification forces chi=6/7. Indeed a nonzero highest-weight-4/3 vector occurs in a unitary representation of charge chi; the allowed minimal-model charges below 9/10 are those with m=3,...,7, and only m=6 has 4/3 in its Kac table.

A sharper finite check gives the next possible charge supporting highest weight 4/3 as 1364/1365 (m=90). For every m<90, the Kac equation is

((m+1)r-ms)^2 = 1+16m(m+1)/3.

The exact finite scan in the certificate has only m=6 below that endpoint and m=90 at it. This is an arithmetic consequence of the imported classification, not an independent proof of the classification. The simpler 9/10 threshold is sufficient for a coarse diagnostic.

Even chi=6/7 plus (5) does not by itself prove that the mixed algebra has exactly the 3A structure. Further closure and cubic checks below remain required. We do not silently turn a useful spectral gap into a full fusion-extension theorem.

## 4. A verified two-dimensional cubic supplies the endpoint

Suppose the products have produced commuting Virasoro vectors u,v of charges 4/5 and 6/7 and a real two-dimensional subspace H, perpendicular to u and v, such that

u*a=(2/3)a,  v*a=(4/3)a for every a in H,

and H*H is contained in span{u,v}+H. The u,v components are fixed by invariance. For an orthonormal basis p,q of H, write the H-valued multiplication tensor as a binary cubic. The tested 3A condition is that it is harmonic,

$$g(x,y)=A(x^3-3xy^2)+B(3x^2y-y^3),\qquad A^2+B^2=\frac{200}{81}.\tag{6}$$

The harmonic conditions and amplitude are checkable algebraic data. They are not assumed to follow merely from the dimension of H or from the existence of the Potts component. Changing the orthonormal basis rotates (A,B) through three times the basis angle, so their squared amplitude is invariant.

On the unit circle, g is one sinusoid of triple angle. It has three positive maxima h_j, separated by 120 degrees, each with value 10sqrt(2)/9. In projective coordinates h proportional to p+tq, its stationary lines solve

$$A t^3-3B t^2-3A t+B=0,\tag{7}$$

with the point at infinity included when appropriate. Choose the sign of each line so its cubic value is positive. No Ising endpoint is an input to this computation.

For each selected unit h_j, let H_j=sqrt(162)h_j. Invariance, (6), and the tangent critical equation give

$$H_j*H_j=270u+504v+20H_j.$$

Then direct multiplication proves

$$e_j=\frac5{32}u+\frac7{16}v+\frac1{32}H_j
=\frac1{1152}(H_j*H_j)+\frac1{72}H_j-\frac5{64}u,\tag{8}$$

with e_j*e_j=2e_j and ||e_j||^2=1/4. These are actual Ising vectors in the original exact VOA. The proof is only the displayed finite multiplication, once the conditions have been verified.

Their pairwise inner products are 13/1024. Thus this extraction gives three NONORTHOGONAL Ising vectors. It does not yet satisfy the orthogonal-pair premise in the Abe-Lam-Yamada classification theorem.

### Recovering the plane from a seed in the known 3A example

The source table [HLY, second paper, Lemma 4.2] has a positive real basis u,v,Y,Z with metric diag(2/5,3/7,162,162) and products

uY=(2/3)Y, uZ=(2/3)Z, vY=(4/3)Y, vZ=(4/3)Z,

Y^2=270u+504v+20Y, Z^2=270u+504v-20Y, YZ=-20Z.

For any y=cY+sZ with c^2+s^2=1, equation (1) gives d=504v. Hence (3) recovers v without an endpoint, and ||d||^2/||y||^4=112/27. If y and the H part of y^2 are independent, they generate the two-dimensional plane. If they are dependent, y already lies on a stationary line; select its sign by the cubic coefficient rather than dividing by zero in a plane-construction step.

A nonstationary rational seed y=(3Y+4Z)/5 is used in the checker. The four vectors u,y,y^2,u*y^2 span a closed four-dimensional algebra. In the associated orthogonal equal-norm plane basis, the cubic coefficients divided by the common squared norm are A=-468/25 and B=-176/25. Their squared amplitude is 400, equivalent to (6) after unit normalization. Equation (7) factors as a nonzero scalar times

(3t+4)(39t^2-96t+11),

so its exact roots are -4/3 and 16/13 +/-25sqrt(3)/39. All three Ising vectors are reconstructed from these stationary lines. The check does not hand an Ising vector to the algorithm and call that discovery.

This is a constructive reorganization of a known local algebra. It is not a theorem that such a plane appears from every seed in the full 1566-dimensional Potts eigenspace, nor an efficient oracle procedure for an unknown VOA.

## 5. A real subtheory where the same closure strategy cannot produce Ising

A second source-realized example is crucial. In the second paper of [HLY], equations (3.14)-(3.15) and Lemma 3.6 identify the ternary-code VOA

$$U=M_D=W(4/5)^{\otimes3}\oplus W[2/3]_+^{\otimes3}\oplus W[2/3]_-^{\otimes3},$$

where D={000,111,(-1,-1,-1)}. Its central charge is 12/5 and its entire weight-two space is five-dimensional. Proposition 5.15, Theorem 5.16 and the Appendix provide its embedding into the moonshine VOA through the larger tetra-code construction. This is a proper subtheory inside the target, not another holomorphic c24 theory.

Its real weight-two basis is u1,u2,u3,Y,Z, with positive diagonal metric

(2/5,2/5,2/5,54,54).

The complete products, from [HLY, Lemma 3.11], are

ui^2=2ui, ui*uj=0 for i!=j,

ui*Y=(2/3)Y, ui*Z=(2/3)Z,

Y^2=90(u1+u2+u3)+8Y,
Z^2=90(u1+u2+u3)-8Y,
YZ=-8Z.

Take u=u1 and the generic seed y=(3Y+4Z)/5. Again, u,y,y^2,u*y^2 generate a CLOSED four-dimensional algebra, now span{u1,u2+u3,Y,Z}. Equation (1) gives d=90(u2+u3), and equation (3) returns

$$v=u2+u3,\qquad \chi=8/5,\qquad
\frac{\|d\|^2}{\|y\|^4}=20/9.$$

Both residuals (5) vanish, so the failure is not an approximate computation or a non-Virasoro companion. Its charge is genuinely different. Its unit-plane cubic is harmonic too, but its squared amplitude is 32/27 rather than 200/81.

The two examples therefore share a Potts component, a two-dimensional coupled plane, positive metric, exact finite closure, and a genuine commuting companion acting with weight 4/3. These facts alone do not force an Ising component. The companion and cubic normalizations are essential.

## 6. A short proof that the whole five-dimensional subtheory is Ising-free

A weight-two Ising vector would have the form

$$e=a_1u_1+a_2u_2+a_3u_3+bY+cZ.$$

Put R2=b^2+c^2 and S=a1+a2+a3. The complete idempotent equation e^2=2e reads

$$a_i^2-a_i+45R2=0\quad(i=1,2,3),\tag{9}$$

$$[(4/3)S-2](b,c)+8(b^2-c^2,-2bc)=0.\tag{10}$$

The conformal vector of U is Omega=u1+u2+u3. Invariance and idempotence give central charge

$$2\langle e,e\rangle=2\langle e,\Omega\rangle=\frac45S.$$

For charge 1/2, S=5/8. If R2=0 in the real case, (9) makes each ai either zero or one, which is impossible. Otherwise each ai is one of two complementary roots in [0,1]. The larger root cannot occur when their sum is 5/8: even one larger root and two smaller ones sum to at least one. Thus all ai=5/24. Equation (9) requires

$$R2=\frac{19}{5184}.$$

Squaring and summing (10) instead gives

$$R2=\frac{[(4/3)(5/8)-2]^2}{64}=\frac{49}{2304}.$$

These are unequal. Therefore no real Ising vector exists anywhere in U2.

### Complete idempotent certificate

For completeness, the same equations can be solved exactly rather than testing only charge 1/2. If R2=0, the eight Boolean triples ai give eight solutions. There are no additional complex solutions with R2=0 and (b,c) nonzero: then c=+/-ib, and the two equations in (10) force the coefficient [(4/3)S-2] to equal both 16b and -16b.

For R2!=0, let a be the smaller root and k the number of ai equal to 1-a. With S=k+(3-2k)a, the radial equation is

$$45[(4/3)S-2]^2=64a(1-a).$$

Its factorizations are 4(14a-9)(14a-5)=0 for k=0,3, and 4(6a-5)(6a-1)=0 for k=1,2. Relabeling the complementary roots leaves a=5/14 or1/6. Equation (10) has exactly three plane phases per choice, namely the triple-angle directions. All solutions lie in the real field Q(sqrt(3)). There are 32 distinct idempotents including zero:

| Central charge | Number |
|---|---:|
| 0 | 1 |
| 4/5 | 3 |
| 6/7 | 3 |
| 14/15 | 9 |
| 22/15 | 9 |
| 54/35 | 3 |
| 8/5 | 3 |
| 12/5 | 1 |

In particular charge 1/2 is absent even among complex solutions of the full weight-two equations. The list is exhaustive by the polynomial elimination above; the finite checker verifies every entry and branch, rather than treating a numerical root search as a proof of completeness. Equivalent algebra classifications may be implicit in prior work; novelty is not established.

Because U is an actual sub-VOA, all successive modes of fields chosen inside it stay in U. Its entire weight-two space has just been checked. Thus even allowing higher intermediate fields from those generators cannot produce an Ising vector. This is stronger than a bad local maximum or a failure of one chosen polynomial ansatz.

Centering with the ambient stress does not remove the obstruction. The complementary stress omega-Omega commutes with U and has charge 108/5. The resulting weight-two algebra is the direct sum U2 plus this stress line; its idempotent coefficient is zero or one, so the possible charges add either zero or108/5 to the listed nonnegative charges. None becomes 1/2. This is not a barrier to using OTHER ambient moonshine fields outside that subtheory.

## 7. What the comparison establishes, and what remains open

The positive result removes a supplied endpoint from a finite construction. Once the right coupled plane has been verified, solving its binary cubic and taking one mixed square produces the Ising directions. One does not need to climb the ambient self-coupling or guess one of those directions beforehand.

The negative result shows that even a genuine small, closed, positive, highly structured mixed sector can remain Ising-free inside moonshine. Thus 'find any closed mixed sector' is not an existence proof. In the examples, the extracted companion charge distinguishes the two possibilities immediately; the full harmonic-cubic conditions make the successful case constructive.

The remaining task is to force an APPROPRIATE seed and closure, or to use a different global principle, from the original holomorphic/current-free hypotheses. Neither holomorphicity of the ambient theory nor the existence of the Potts eigenspace transfers holomorphicity or Ising vectors to every subtheory. The obstruction supplied here is not evidence of another holomorphic c24 candidate, and does not refute moonshine uniqueness.

A charge-6/7 Virasoro vector also occurs elsewhere in the Ising-free code algebra (as the histogram shows). Therefore finding an isolated charge-6/7 component is not enough either: its particular compatibility with u and the mixed plane must be retained. The weak candidate-charge diagnostic should not be advertised as a new sufficient classification theorem.

The three Ising vectors in the successful extraction are nonorthogonal. The earlier two-orthogonal-Ising classification endpoint remains a separate obligation. No first-Ising existence theorem, orthogonal-partner theorem, or full physical selection has been proved in this continuation.
